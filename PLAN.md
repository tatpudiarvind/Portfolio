- Preserve responsive layouts, semantic structure, keyboard focus, native disclosures, and reduced-motion support.
- Keep current Go/Kubernetes work labeled as in progress and claim no result for it.
- On this public site, show Siemens outcome numbers but keep internal product and system names and Siemens counts general (Arvind's decision, 10 November 2026).
